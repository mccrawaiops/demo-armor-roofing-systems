About

Armor Roofing Systems, Inc.

Armor Roofing Systems is a family-owned roofing company proudly serving Loveland, Fort Collins, Greeley, and communities throughout Northern Colorado.

Armor Roofing Systems was started by Larry Wilson’s father when he was just 24 years old. Today, the company is led by Larry with support from his son, Blake. Throughout the years, our goal has remained simple: provide quality roofing services at a fair price while treating every customer with honesty and respect.

As a local, family-run company, we care about the people and communities we serve. We prioritize lasting relationships over maximizing profits and take the time to understand what each customer needs. Our team communicates clearly throughout the project and works hard to keep every job on schedule and within the agreed-upon budget.
